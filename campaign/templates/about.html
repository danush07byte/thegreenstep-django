What is Climate Action?

SDG 13: Climate Action is one of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals. It encourages people, communities, and countries to take urgent action to reduce climate change and protect the environment.

Main Objectives

  • Reduce greenhouse gas emissions.
  • Promote renewable energy.
  • Encourage recycling and waste reduction.
  • Protect forests, rivers, and natural ecosystems.
  • Educate communities about climate change.

Climate Issues in Malaysia

Malaysia faces climate-related problems such as floods, rising temperatures, heavy rainfall, deforestation, and pollution. These issues affect homes, health, agriculture, and daily life.

How The Green Step Helps

The Green Step promotes awareness by sharing climate information and encouraging people to join volunteer activities such as tree planting, recycling campaigns, and environmental awareness programmes.
